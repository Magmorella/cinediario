How to use it

  1. Download CineDiario.exe and double-click it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info and then Run anyway: the warning appears because the app has no paid digital signature.
  2. A black window opens (keep it open), followed by your browser with the app.
  3. Create a free account on themoviedb.org, request your key under Settings → API and paste it into the app. You only do this once.
  4. Search for a movie, open it and add it to your list.

Your movies stay on your PC only, in the Documents\CineDiario folder.
